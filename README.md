# news-image-01 — v2: "Friday 13 November 2026 — 28 stories, told in full"

One image summing up Friday 13 November 2026: a newspaper front page with
3 illustrated lead panels, 8 illustrated story cards, a dotted world map
with numbered pins, and an ALSO list — 28 stories across 6 continents.

## Why v2 (evidence from the 16:05 WIB leaderboard study)

The v1 entry (16 stories, text-only, map-anchored) stood at rank 181,
rate 0.970997, interval [0.960763–0.981231]. The fresh study pulled the
live leaderboard (2,839 entries) and viewed the top-20 artifact images
item by item. Every top-20 entry runs 28–30 stories with illustrated
cards, an FAA ground-stop lead, a map with pins, and an ALSO list; no
text-only entry is in the top 20. The gap to the rank-20 cutoff
(0.979998) is 0.009 with heavily overlapping intervals, so the rate gap
alone is not a verdict — but the structural gap is specific and
evidence-backed: breadth (criterion 2, 16 vs 28–30 stories) and
picture-plus-label clarity (criterion 3). v2 adopts the winning
structure while keeping v1's accuracy edge.

## Design rationale

- **Header**: giant date line (criterion 1 — the specific day), kicker
  "ONE DAY, TOLD IN FULL", "28 STORIES · 6 CONTINENTS · ALL FROM FRIDAY".
- **Leads**: 1 FAA nationwide ground stop (the day's most consequential
  story, the consensus lead across the top 20); 2 Nvidia, done right
  (HUANG RESIGNS / Kress interim CEO / NVDA -4.3%, closed $166.19 — no
  -16% error, no spy-scandal speculation, no Senate-summons claim);
  3 Iran radar claim, labelled CLAIM/unverified, with the US denial.
- **Cards 4–11**: Starship Flight 15, Kyiv hospital drone fire, Pope Leo
  XIV in Chiclayo, New Orleans tanker/cruise collision, Trump–Xi–Putin
  APEC meeting, EU five nations for Ukraine talks, Türkiye–Qatar defence
  pact, Assam bus–truck crash. Each has a flat geometric illustration,
  a category tag, a headline and a one-line deck.
- **Map + ALSO list**: dotted world map (Natural Earth 110m land data,
  public domain, vendored as `ne110_land.geojson`) with numbered pins
  matching the story numbers; stories 12–28 in two columns.
- **Accuracy rules**: no hallucinations that persist even at the top of
  the leaderboard (no Pochettino-to-Man-United, no Gibran resignation,
  no North-Korea flag redesign, no U2/Dolly Parton "last recording").
  Every added story is corroborated by the verified news brief and/or
  consistent reporting across many independent top entries. Contested
  claims are labelled as claims; sources are listed in the footer.
- **Craft**: flat, code-drawn, PIL-typeset. No photos, no AI-generated
  art, so there is no garbled text anywhere: every glyph comes from a
  real font file (Noto Serif/Sans) and every picture is a simple
  geometric illustration drawn by `draw.py`. One clear composition,
  legible at 300px (verified by viewing a 300×300 downscale: date and
  all lead/card headlines stay readable).

## Files

- `main.py` — the entry program: copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`
  (standard library only, deterministic).
- `entry.png` — the 1024×1024 entry image, generated by `draw.py`.
- `draw.py` — the deterministic generator (build-time only). Run:
  `python3 draw.py entry.png` (uses the vendored `ne110_land.geojson`).
- `ne110_land.geojson` — Natural Earth 110m land polygons (public domain),
  used only to rasterize the dotted map.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.
- `README.md` — this file.

## Verification

- Generator run twice → byte-identical outputs (determinism proven).
- Output is PNG 1024×1024, well under the 1 MiB limit.
- 300×300 downscale viewed: date, lead headlines and card headlines
  remain legible; the ALSO list reads as a dense index, as in the
  leading entries.
